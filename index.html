FAQ

Common questions from families like yours.

How does the free consultation work?

The free consultation is a 30-minute video call where we get to know your student: their academic background, extracurricular activities, college targets, and timeline. We'll give you an honest assessment of where they stand and recommend the right tier of service. There is absolutely no obligation.

Do you guarantee admission to specific schools?

No, and we never will — and you should be cautious of any service that does. College admissions is a holistic, unpredictable process. What we guarantee is our full effort, honest guidance, and our best judgment applied to your student's specific situation. Our goal is to help your student put forward the strongest possible application.

How do sessions work — in person or virtual?

All sessions are conducted via video call (Zoom or Google Meet), which means we can work with students anywhere. Between sessions, we're available via email or messaging for quick questions and to review drafts.

When is the best time to start?

For most families, summer before senior year is the ideal window. There's time to develop a strong strategy before the fall application season begins. However, we work with students at all stages, including those who are already in the middle of writing. Reach out and we'll tell you honestly what's achievable given your timeline.

How many students do you work with at once?

We deliberately cap our client load to ensure every student receives our full attention. We are not a volume-based service. Availability is limited each cycle, which is why we recommend booking a consultation as early as possible to secure a spot.
